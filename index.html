My journey

From the hills of Nepal to building real hardware.

I grew up in the mountains of Nepal, where limited infrastructure made engineering feel personal. As a child, I remember walking downhill to the river and back uphill carrying water to my village. Later, seeing how water could also power hydroturbines and provide electricity showed me how practical systems can change everyday life.

That experience shaped my path from Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering in India to my M.S. in Applied Energy and Electromechanical Engineering at UNC Charlotte. Today, I focus on mechanical design, thermal systems, electromechanical hardware, prototyping, testing, and manufacturing support.
