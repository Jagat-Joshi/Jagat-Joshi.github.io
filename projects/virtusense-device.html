Engineering problem

Challenge & design intent

Problem

Compact electronic hardware required mechanical packaging and thermal solutions that could be rapidly prototyped and evaluated before production.

Design intent

Develop an enclosure and heat-sink solution, verify fit/function quickly through additive manufacturing, and support product configuration and BOM discipline.

Constraints

  • Compact electronics packaging
  • Thermal behavior
  • Fit/function validation
  • New CAD environment
  • Prototype speed
  • Product integration requirements
Role

What I owned

  • Adopted Fusion 360 in a new product domain.
  • Developed a heat-sink solution for electronic hardware.
  • Designed electronic housings and used 3D-printed prototypes for fit/function checks.
  • Supported BOM development and product configuration for a new alert system.
  • Participated in prototype evaluation and hardware integration.
Why this project matters

This project demonstrates my preferred engineering workflow: connect system requirements to mechanical design, move the design into real hardware, observe actual behavior, and use evidence from fabrication or testing to guide the next iteration.
