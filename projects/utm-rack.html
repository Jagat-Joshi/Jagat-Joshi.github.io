Engineering problem

Challenge & design intent

Problem

Multiple instruments and modules needed to be integrated into one mobile rack without compromising structural integrity, access, cable routing, airflow, mobility, or serviceability.

Design intent

Develop a complete rack architecture that technicians could fabricate, assemble, operate, move, and service as an integrated test system.

Constraints

  • Fixed instrument envelopes
  • Structural loading
  • Front/rear access
  • Cable routing
  • Ventilation
  • Display ergonomics
  • Caster/mobility requirements
  • Evolving component list
Role

What I owned

  • Developed rack architecture and equipment layout in SolidWorks.
  • Designed structural members, panels, supports, drawers, clamps, and mounting interfaces.
  • Created and maintained component/BOM information during design evolution.
  • Worked directly with manufacturing to resolve fit-up and integration issues.
  • Supported assembly of instruments and mechanical subassemblies into a working system.
Why this project matters

This project demonstrates my preferred engineering workflow: connect system requirements to mechanical design, move the design into real hardware, observe actual behavior, and use evidence from fabrication or testing to guide the next iteration.
