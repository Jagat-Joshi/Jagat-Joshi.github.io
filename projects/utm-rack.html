The Problem

A multidisciplinary hardware stack needed a robust structure that could package electronics and test hardware while remaining buildable and serviceable.

Design Intent

Create a clean mechanical architecture that supported installation, cable routing, access, cooling, and future revisions.

What I Did

Designed the complete rack in CAD, prepared drawings and BOMs, coordinated fabrication, supported installation and assembly, performed fit verification, and updated the design based on real build feedback.

Why It Matters

This project reflects full-cycle ownership: concept, CAD, documentation, fabrication, assembly, fit checks, troubleshooting, and revision.
