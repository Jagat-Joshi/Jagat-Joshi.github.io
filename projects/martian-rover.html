Engineering problem

Challenge & design intent

Problem

A four-person team needed to design and fabricate a tele-operated rover capable of traversing representative rough terrain using available student manufacturing resources.

Design intent

Create a lightweight rover with sufficient articulation, ground clearance, alignment, torque capacity, and structural robustness to negotiate rocks, bumps, and step-like obstacles.

Constraints

  • Target mass below 8 kg
  • Minimum ground clearance 40 mm
  • Target 50 mm step/bump
  • Motor and wheel packaging
  • Alignment
  • Limited build time and available materials
Role

What I owned

  • Designed chassis/suspension components and mechanical interfaces in SolidWorks/CATIA.
  • Used ANSYS-based analysis and considered static/dynamic loads, torque, articulation, and clearances.
  • Fabricated components using laser cutting, sheet-metal punching/bending, machining, welding, and assembly.
  • Integrated mechanical and electrical subsystems and performed fit checks.
  • Physically validated the rover on rocks, bumps, step-like obstacles, and loose/uneven terrain.
Why this project matters

This project demonstrates my preferred engineering workflow: connect system requirements to mechanical design, move the design into real hardware, observe actual behavior, and use evidence from fabrication or testing to guide the next iteration.
