Counter-Drone Electronics Thermal Redesign

Improve reliability of RF/electronic hardware experiencing heat buildup and component failures.

← All Projects

The Problem

Prototype hardware showed significant internal heat buildup and repeated electronics/IC failures.

Design Intent

Reduce thermal stress while preserving RF/electronic packaging, serviceability, mounting interfaces, and system constraints.

What I Did

Reviewed internal layout and heat-transfer paths, supported thermal analysis, redesigned enclosure/cooling features and component arrangement, and coordinated changes with RF, electrical, and manufacturing teams.

Engineering Tradeoffs

Cooling performance had to be balanced against enclosure size, cable routing, mounting, fabrication constraints, and access for maintenance.

Outcome

Improved thermal stability and reduced the risk of repeat heat-related failure through a combination of mechanical redesign and better thermal pathways.

Tools

SolidWorks, thermal analysis, electronics packaging, DFM, prototype testing
