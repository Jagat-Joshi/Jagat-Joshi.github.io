Engineering problem

Challenge & design intent

Problem

The facility required a structured review of major energy uses and practical conservation opportunities that could be implemented with reasonable payback.

Design intent

Evaluate building systems and operating behavior, then identify technically practical conservation measures and estimate savings/payback.

Constraints

  • Existing building operation
  • HVAC and ventilation requirements
  • Lighting schedules
  • Utility data quality
  • Implementation practicality
Role

What I owned

  • Reviewed HVAC, ventilation, lighting, operating schedules, and utility use.
  • Evaluated thermostat scheduling, demand-controlled ventilation, occupancy/daylight sensing, and related measures.
  • Calculated savings and simple-payback estimates.
  • Synthesized results into actionable energy-conservation recommendations.
Why this project matters

This project demonstrates my preferred engineering workflow: connect system requirements to mechanical design, move the design into real hardware, observe actual behavior, and use evidence from fabrication or testing to guide the next iteration.
