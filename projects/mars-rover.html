Martian Soil Excavation Rover

Design and fabricate a tele-operated rover capable of rough-terrain mobility and soil-excavation support.

The Problem

The rover needed to remain compact and light while navigating rough terrain and integrating mechanical and electrical subsystems.

What I Did

Contributed to chassis, suspension, wheels, motor mounts, CAD development, structural checks, fabrication, electrical/mechanical integration, and physical testing.

Constraints

Target mass below 8 kg, approximately 40 mm minimum ground clearance, and the ability to handle roughly 50 mm steps/bumps.

Outcome

Produced a working fabricated prototype through a combination of CAD, analysis, laser cutting, sheet forming, machining, welding, assembly, and test.

Tools

SolidWorks, CATIA, ANSYS, fabrication, robotics hardware
