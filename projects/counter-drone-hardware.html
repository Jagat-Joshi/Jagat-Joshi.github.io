Engineering problem

Challenge & design intent

Problem

The platform had to package RF electronics, computing hardware, radar/detector-related modules, cabling, cooling hardware, and service interfaces. Prototype heat buildup contributed to component instability and failures.

Design intent

Create mechanically robust, serviceable packaging that preserved RF/electrical interfaces while improving thermal stability and supporting fabrication and integration.

Constraints

  • Existing PCB/RF geometry and connector locations
  • Tight packaging
  • Heat-generating electronics
  • Multiple frequency-specific housings
  • Service access
  • Manufacturability
  • Cross-disciplinary interface requirements
Role

What I owned

  • Designed structural stands, enclosures, mounting interfaces, and equipment packaging in SolidWorks.
  • Created detailed models, drawings, BOMs, and revisions around RF/electrical constraints.
  • Reviewed component layout and heat-transfer paths and supported thermal analysis.
  • Revised enclosure geometry and cooling approach based on prototype behavior.
  • Supported prototype assembly with RF, electrical, manufacturing, and technician teams.
Why this project matters

This project demonstrates my preferred engineering workflow: connect system requirements to mechanical design, move the design into real hardware, observe actual behavior, and use evidence from fabrication or testing to guide the next iteration.
