Engineering problem

Challenge & design intent

Problem

A large school retrofit required a lower-carbon heating/cooling concept that could integrate source-side heat exchange with building-side ventilation and hydronic distribution.

Design intent

Develop a district-style thermal architecture and examine equipment interfaces, heat-pump integration, ventilation, hydronics, and COP implications.

Constraints

  • Retrofit context
  • Source-side heat exchange
  • Ventilation needs
  • Hydronic distribution
  • Equipment interfaces
  • System-level efficiency
Role

What I owned

  • Developed the thermal-system concept for the school retrofit.
  • Evaluated river-source heat exchange, plate heat exchanger interfaces, water-source heat pumps, and DOAS.
  • Considered hydronic distribution and major equipment interfaces.
  • Compared system-level energy and COP implications.
Why this project matters

This project demonstrates my preferred engineering workflow: connect system requirements to mechanical design, move the design into real hardware, observe actual behavior, and use evidence from fabrication or testing to guide the next iteration.

Outcome

Result

Produced a coherent system architecture linking source-side heat exchange, water-source heat pumps, dedicated outdoor air, and hydronic distribution for a large building retrofit.
